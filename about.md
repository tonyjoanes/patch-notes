Patch Notes is a weekday digest of actively exploited and notable vulnerabilities, written for .NET, Azure, container and DevOps teams. Each item includes a short description of the issue, who should care, a recommended fix, and a source link.

The briefings are written with AI assistance and reviewed by [Tony Joanes](https://github.com/tonyjoanes).

This is not professional security advice. Always verify the details against the vendor advisory before you act.
